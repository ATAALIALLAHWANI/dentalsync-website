What is DentalSync?

One simple system for your whole clinic's daily workflow

DentalSync is a dental clinic management system built for dentists and clinic teams. It brings your patients, appointments, and treatment documentation into one place — so nothing lives on scattered paper files or sticky notes.

It's designed to be simple enough for your front desk to use on day one, in either Arabic or English.

  • Every patient, appointment, and visit in one dashboard
  • Find any patient's full history in seconds
  • Works in Arabic and English, side by side
What can DentalSync do?

Everything your clinic does day to day, organized

The real features inside DentalSync today — nothing more, nothing invented.

Patient Records

Keep every patient's name, age, and contact details organized in one searchable list — with a full profile just a click away.

Patient Search

Search for any patient by name and get straight to their file — no more digging through drawers or paper folders.

Appointments

Book appointments with patient, date, time, type, and duration — and see Today, Upcoming, and Past at a glance.

Voice Data Entry

Speak naturally during the visit and let DentalSync's AI turn it into a structured clinical note for you.

Visit History

Every past visit is saved to the patient's profile — chief complaint, diagnosis, treatment plan, and clinical findings, in order.

PDF Export

Export a clean, ready-to-share visit report as a PDF, straight from the patient's visit history.

The flagship feature

Your Voice. Your Notes. Less Typing.

Instead of typing up every visit by hand, describe it out loud the way you normally would — DentalSync's AI listens and turns it into an organized clinical note.

Speak

Process
